# Update — Specialization returns

**You can specialize your skills again.** Visit a Temple to specialize a skill.

Skill credits for leveling have been returned, and that's the whole pool — about 46 by the level cap. The credits you started with aren't part of it: those are what pay for every skill being trained for free, which is also why training costs nothing and untraining refunds nothing.

Two places retail will mislead you. Uncapped ranks removed specialization's old ceiling advantage, so a specialized skill **grows faster** rather than reaching higher. And unspecializing refunds the credits in full, so changing your mind costs nothing.

**Quests now feed your attributes.** Completing a quest gives a small push to all six. A low attribute gains the most, tapering off as it climbs — so the ones you've neglected catch up fastest. It won't out-pace grinding, and it isn't meant to: it's so that time spent on quests is no longer time spent falling behind.

**You can set aside skills you never use.** Untrain a skill and your buff cycles get shorter. Nothing is lost — its progress is frozen exactly where it was, and re-training it costs nothing and restores it in full, whenever you want.
