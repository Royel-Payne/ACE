# Update — Tinkering can be levelled

**Salvaging now trains the tinkering skills.** Every salvage pays the matching tinkering skill as well as Salvaging itself, and the material decides which one learns — bronze and steel teach Armor Tinkering, iron and granite teach Weapon Tinkering, copper and pyreal teach Item Tinkering, and the semi-precious gems teach Magic Item Tinkering.

That is the path, and there wasn't one before. The four tinkering skills could only be raised by tinkering, and tinkering awarded nothing at all — so they sat wherever character creation left them, no matter how much you used them.

**Salvaging itself is about three times faster, and no longer stalls.** Its difficulty used to come from workmanship alone, which topped out at 80 while the skill kept climbing — so past that point every rank cost more and paid less, and salvaging quietly stopped going anywhere around rank 100. Difficulty now scales with the material too, the same way tinkering does, so the high-workmanship materials that used to be worth least are worth most.

**A successful tinker pays experience as well.** Failures pay nothing, and that is deliberate — a failed tink already destroys the salvage, and paying for it would make failing a strategy. Treat it as a reward for the work rather than the route to it. Salvaging is the route.

**Attributes were never capped at 200.** If yours announced it had "reached its upper limit" somewhere in the low 200s, it hadn't — it kept climbing the whole time, and the message was simply firing about 90 ranks early. The ceiling is 290, as it always was. The celebration now happens once, when you actually get there.
