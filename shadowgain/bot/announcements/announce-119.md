# Update — two gain rules tightened, and it came from you

**Very hard targets no longer pay out of all proportion.** Skill gain has always scaled with how hard a thing is relative to your own skill. What it never did was *stop* scaling — so a target far beyond you paid without limit, and a very low skill swinging at something enormous paid best of all. Gain is now bounded relative to your own skill, so the ceiling rises as you do.

**An appropriately hard fight pays exactly what it paid yesterday.** This is not a nerf to normal play, and that is measured rather than hoped: replayed against every skill award earned on the server in the last 24 hours, **99.7% come out identical to the point**, and the total experience earned across the day moves by less than a tenth of a percent. The awards that change are the handful that were never in proportion to begin with.

**Fighting another player no longer trains anything.** Skills, attributes and combat specialties were all open to it, in both directions — hitting and being hit.

**Nobody lost a rank.** Nothing you have earned moved; this changes a rate, not a curve. Verified across every character on the shard, either side of the change.

**This came from you.** **Apex**, **Jkurs**, **Greylock** and **Adramelech** worked these out and compared notes openly in #chat rather than quietly using them — and Jkurs asked for one of his own to be closed. That is the whole reason it is fixed tonight, and it is a better way to run a server than me guessing. Thank you, genuinely.
