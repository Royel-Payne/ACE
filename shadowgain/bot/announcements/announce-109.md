# Update — Skills no longer run out

**A skill can no longer run out of experience.** Skills were meant to be unlimited, but there was a hard ceiling in the way progress was stored, and anything earned past it was quietly discarded. A couple of skills on the server had already reached it and had been earning nothing for days. That ceiling is gone, and they are earning again.

**Past the top of the skill table, the climb now continues the same curve.** The cost of a rank beyond the table used to drop sharply — so the very end of a skill was far easier than the long stretch before it. It now carries on rising exactly as it did on the way up: rank 209 costs a little more than 208 did, the same way 208 cost more than 207. Nothing at or below the table top changed.

**Your skill panel cannot count that high.** Once a skill passes about 4.29 billion experience the panel stops counting, because the client physically cannot display a larger number. `@myskills` shows the real total and what the next rank actually costs — for a high skill, that command is now the honest view and the panel is not.

That command exists because **Apex** asked for it, and it earned its keep faster than expected: it is what made all of the above visible in the first place. Good call.

**Loyalty and Leadership climb more slowly.** They scaled with everything your allegiance earned rather than with any difficulty of their own, so they were racing ahead of skills that take real work. They were always the least finished part of the system, and this is a first correction rather than a final one.

**A small number of ranks have been corrected.** A few skills were showing ranks that the experience behind them did not actually pay for. The experience itself is untouched, and those skills keep earning from where they are.

Found something broken? `/bug` here, or `@bug` in game.
