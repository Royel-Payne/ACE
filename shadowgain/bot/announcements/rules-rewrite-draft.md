# DRAFT — replacement for #rules-and-setup

Not posted. Written for review as a diff, per the 121 convention.

The problem this fixes, in Jkurs's own words on 2026-08-16:

> why are you making a server ruleset to enforce competitive play but then not enforcing
> competitive play?

He is describing a real mismatch, not a grievance. The current rules promise enforcement
("we may ask you to restart your character") and the server is run as a hobby. Someone who
reads the rules and believes them will eventually feel let down, and that is what happened.

---

## What is changing and why

| current line | problem |
|---|---|
| "Do not exploit them when found" | implies a competitive integrity worth policing, and casts a bug-finder as a suspect |
| "we may ask you to restart your character" | a punishment that has never been used and that Chris did not want to use even here - a threat you will not carry out costs more than no rule at all |
| "We keep things fair" | "fair" invites the reading that there is a contest to be fair *about* |

Nothing here removes the two rules that are actually load-bearing: don't macro somewhere
that ruins the place for others, and tell us when something is broken.

---

## PROPOSED TEXT

**Shadowgain is a hobby server. Please treat it like one.**

This is a small server run for fun in someone's spare time. It is not a competition, there is
no prize, and nobody is keeping score in a way that should affect how you play. The Honour
Roll is there to celebrate people who took the slow road, not to rank you against anyone.

**If you find something broken, tell us.** Bugs are expected — the whole progression system
here is homemade and unfinished. Reporting one is a favour to everyone, and you will never be
punished for having found it or for having used it before anyone knew it was a bug. We fix
the hole; we do not go looking for someone to blame.

**Macroing is fine, including unattended.** Please just do it somewhere quiet rather than in
a busy spot where it spoils the area for other people.

**One active game session per connection.** Own as many accounts as you like — the limit is
only on how many can be logged in at once from the same connection. If you genuinely share a
household or connection with another player, that is fine: ask a @Moderator and we will set
up an exception.

**Be decent to each other.** That is the only rule with any teeth. Bring the fun down for
other people and you will be asked to stop; keep at it and you will be asked to leave. What
someone else's character has, how fast they levelled, and which path they took is not worth
anyone's evening.

a public leaderboard plus a badge for reaching the top. Options, from lightest to heaviest:

- keep the roll but drop the ORDERING - show it as a list of people on the long road rather
  than ranked by level, which removes "who is winning" without removing the recognition
- keep both exactly as they are and accept that some players will treat it as a race
