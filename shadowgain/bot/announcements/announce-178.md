# Update — crafting has a real path, and lockpicking works

**The crafting skills have never been worth training here, and that was the numbers, not you.** Measured across everyone above level 50: not a single character had gained one rank of Cooking. Alchemy averaged 3, Lockpick 3, Fletching 9.

**Crafting turn-ins now pay in ranks, not experience.** The Crafting Experience Tasks are unchanged in how you use them — craft something, hand it to the collector, get paid. What changed is what "paid" means: the reward is a share of the rank you are actually on, so it keeps pace with you instead of falling behind. A skill sitting at zero climbs fast; one that is already healthy moves a little. Tasks exist for Alchemy, Cooking, Fletching and all four tinkering skills. Salvaging is unchanged — it already worked.

**The turn-in lockout is down from 20 hours to 1 hour.** These are meant to be enjoyable side skills, not a second job. If you want to spend a weekend pushing Cooking, that is allowed now.

**Lockpicking had the same problem for a different reason, and was the worst off of the lot.** Picking now pays properly, and above the point where the old rate started falling behind it switches to the same rank-based reward the crafts use.

**No lock anywhere got harder to pick.** That would have meant doors you could no longer open, which was never worth it. Harder locks are still worth more than easy ones, so a real chest beats a house door — but nothing was taken away.

**Evading attacks now trains Quickness**, as a secondary gain alongside Coordination. Nothing was removed; this is added on top, and it is deliberately a steady trickle rather than a windfall.

**Health no longer waits on Endurance the way it did.** Endurance was the only real route to a bigger health pool and it climbed too slowly to keep up with the content, which left higher tiers gated behind a number nobody could reach. That link is loosened.

**Summoning now trains Endurance**, giving it a second path.

**`@sg-train <skill>` re-trains a skill you have pruned.** Skill credits were never actually required for this — the client just would not let you click the button when you had none. Your ranks and experience were always preserved; this gives you the way back in.

**A cooking collector in Uziz has been standing in the wrong place since 2022.** It was a duplicate of the Al-Arqas one, sharing its quest, so it could never actually pay you. It is now the collector that was supposed to be there.

**Everything above is tunable while the server runs.** If any of it feels wrong, say so in #bugs or just tell me — none of these numbers are precious, and several are first guesses that will move once we see them in use.
