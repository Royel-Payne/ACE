# Update — crafting is worth doing, and two augmentations that did nothing now work

**Crafting turn-ins have been broken in three separate ways, and all three are fixed.** Turn-ins have credited **no character level at all** since the crafting update went in — the reward landed in your skill and stopped there. Seven collectors were also paying a fraction of what the other forty-four paid, and the tier ladder had been flattened so a top-tier trophy paid the same as a bottom-tier one. Turn-ins now credit level like everything else, every collector pays on the same scale, and better trophies are worth more again.

**Raw crafting materials drop six times more often.** The turn-in items were never the problem — you *craft* those, and the raw materials they are made from sat at a 5% drop rate. That is now **30%**. I ran the numbers against real kill rates before picking it: this was meant to be an enjoyable side activity, not another grind with thorns in it.

**Aetheria, cloaks and rare gear level up again.** Equipped levelling gear fed on kill experience, and when kills stopped granting character experience that feed went silent — fifteen of the forty levelling items on the server were sitting at exactly zero. Item experience now comes from **skill use**, as an additional pool: nothing is taken from you, and it keeps working the way the rest of your progression does.

**Two augmentations that were doing literally nothing now work.**

The **Augmented Understanding** trinkets (the Pathwarden trinket every character gets free, and the imbued versions) boost experience — but only on the paths that still existed. Since kills stopped granting levels, that was quest turn-ins and nothing else. They now boost **skill and attribute gain** as well, which is where your level actually comes from. That is 4% for the free trinket, 6% for a Major imbue.

**Quick Learner was worse: it was worth exactly 0%.** It only ever applied to creature kills, so it has been giving nothing at all since the levelling change — while still costing **4 billion** experience, unrepeatable. It now pays its 5% on skill and attribute gain. It still does not affect quest experience or trophy turn-ins, exactly as its description says.

*One thing worth knowing before you spend a Major imbue: the three tiers do NOT stack. Only the highest counts, so Understanding I + II + III on one trinket pays 6%, not 12%. Use the other two imbue slots for something else.*

**Fellowships now grant bonus skill experience.** Unifying levels removed the reason to group — the point of a fellowship was sharing kill experience, and kill experience no longer drives level. This gives grouping a reason again **without** restoring a share: it multiplies your *own* skill and attribute gain and never pools or transfers anything, so nobody levels off someone else's practice. A duo is worth about 4%, rising to 10% for a full fellowship. It counts only real people standing near you — your own alts do not count.

**New, and experimental: armour Attribute Set transfer.** Tailors now sell an **Armor Set Extraction Tool** for 50 trade notes. Use it on a piece of armour to pull out its attribute set, producing an applicator that can move that set onto another piece covering the same area. It needs Armor Tinkering and it **can fail** — on failure the piece you used it on is destroyed, so check the success chance in the dialog before you commit. Applying is safe; the target is never at risk. This one is new and deliberately risky, so tell me how it behaves.

**The collectors have stopped lying about their cooldown.** Several were telling you to return "in a week" when the actual lockout is **one hour**. The timer was always right; the message was written before the timer was and never followed it. They now say "later" instead, which stays true whatever the cooldown is set to.

As always — #bugs for anything broken, or just tell me.
