# Update — magic gets a fair rate, and Assess Person works again

**Assess Person has been broken since Thursday, and is fixed.** The change that stopped players training skills off each other was too broad: appraising another player is the *only* way Assess Person is ever trained, so the skill did not slow down, it stopped entirely. If yours has not moved in two days, that is why — and it will move again now. Nothing was lost; the experience you had is exactly where you left it.

**War Magic and Void Magic now pay properly.** A spell's difficulty comes from the target's magic defence, and on the content people actually fight that runs well below its physical defence — so a cast was earning about half what a swing of a two-hander earned, for the same fight. That gap was measured, not guessed, and the two offensive schools are now paid to match. The other schools were not behind and have not changed.

**Mana Conversion now trains Self.** Self was the governing attribute of nothing you actually choose to do — every school trained Focus, and Self only came along at a quarter weight. It now leads on Mana Conversion, so a caster's two mental attributes climb together instead of one dragging.

**Spells cast by your gear no longer train your magic skills.** A proc on a cape or a rune on a weapon is the item acting, not you — so War Magic was rising just from wearing the right thing and swinging. Thanks to **Jkurs** for spotting it and saying so.

**Nobody lost a rank, an attribute point, or any experience.** These change what things earn from here, not what you have earned.
