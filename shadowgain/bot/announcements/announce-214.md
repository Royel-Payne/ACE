# Update — move any armour set anywhere, if you dare

**The tailors now sell a second, yellow kit: the Superior Armor Set Extraction Kit (50 trade notes).** Where the red kit moves an attribute set between two pieces covering the same area, the superior kit draws a set that can be bound onto **any** piece of armour at all — a breastplate's set onto a helm, leggings onto gauntlets, whatever you're building toward.

**It is brutally hard, and that is the entire point.** Extracting with the superior kit demands an Armor Tinkering skill beyond anything reachable with ordinary buffs — practically speaking, you will need a rare (a Smithy's Crystal, or Prodigal Focus and Endurance together) burning while you work, and a failed extraction **destroys the armour you're drawing from**. Then binding the extracted set to its new home succeeds at best **one time in three** — 38% with the Charmed Smith augmentation — and a failed bind **destroys the piece you were applying it to**, applicator and all. Check the success chance in the dialog before you commit; it will tell you the truth.

**Both kits are now single-use.** They're consumed with every attempt, like the tailoring kits they're modelled on — including the red kits already in your packs. One purchase, one attempt. At 50 trade notes the kit is the cheap part of the gamble; the armour is the stake.

**The red same-coverage kit is unchanged otherwise** — its extraction odds and its guaranteed, risk-free application are exactly as they were.

This one is deliberately a prestige gamble: everything about it can eat something you care about, and the ceiling never moves no matter how good you get. Go in with your eyes open — and tell me how it feels.
