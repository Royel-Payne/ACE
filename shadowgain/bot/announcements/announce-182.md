# Update — two logins, mansions, and the fast lane closes

**You can now be logged in twice from the same connection.** The limit was one; it is two as of this morning, and it is already live. It is counted per connection rather than per player, so a genuine two-person household shares the two — if that is you, ask and we will lift it for your connection specifically. The rules channel has been updated to match.

**Mansions no longer require an allegiance rank.** Buying one asked for allegiance rank 6, which is a large-server number: the rank formula needs something like sixty followers arranged just so, and nobody here is close. That requirement is gone. You still need to be level 50 and the monarch of an allegiance, and the purchase price is unchanged — but if you are a monarch sitting on the cash, the mansion is now buyable. Cottages, villas and apartments were never affected; they have no allegiance requirement at all.

**The fast lane is closed.** `@masochist off` will no longer switch you onto it. In practice this takes nothing away: both lanes have been running at the same speed for a little while now, so the only thing it still did was cost you something permanently in exchange for nothing. Three of you asked for it to go, and it has gone. If you are already on it you stay there, and `@masochist on` still works if you want to come back to the hard road.

**The dagger is retiring with it.** The † in front of your name meant you had never taken the fast lane — a choice you were re-making every day you did not take it. With the lane closed that choice no longer exists, and every new character would inherit the mark without ever being offered the decision it stood for. So it comes off rather than quietly becoming meaningless. Nothing about your character changes; it is a name prefix and nothing else.

**Nothing you have earned has changed.** No skills, ranks, attributes or experience were touched by any of this.

As always — if something reads wrong or behaves oddly, say so in #bugs or just tell me. Several of these numbers are first guesses and they move when we see them in use.
