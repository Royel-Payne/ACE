# Update — your level now means what your skills say it means

**Character level is no longer earned by killing things. It is earned by using your skills.** Every point of skill and attribute experience you earn now also counts toward your level, and kills no longer grant level experience directly — though killing still advances you, because killing *is* using your skills. The short version: you can no longer be a high level with beginner skills, because the level *is* the skills.

**Your level has been recalculated to match what you have actually trained, and for a lot of you it went down.** This is the part that will sting, so I want to be straight about it. Adramelech went from 275 to 183. Apex from 252 to 172. Black Breath from 208 to 160. Those are the three highest characters on the server and they took the three biggest cuts, because the old system let level run a long way ahead of skills — Adramelech was level 275 with about a tenth of the skill experience that number implied.

**But a third of you went UP.** Dread gained 36 levels. Vauxwell gained 22. Zan 14, Memento Mori 12, Gredaline, Royel and Trees' all gained. If you have been grinding skills rather than chasing levels, the new number is higher than the old one, and it always should have been. Across 79 characters: 46 down, 32 up, one unchanged.

**Nothing you earned was deleted.** Every skill, every attribute, every rank, every piece of gear is exactly where it was. Your level is now derived from those numbers instead of tracked separately, so what changed is the label, not the character underneath it.

**Your unassigned experience pool was reset to zero, and this one is a real loss.** That pool was filled by kill experience under the old rules, and under the new ones it is filled by quests and by allegiance pass-up instead — it buys augmentation gems and nothing else. Carrying the old balance forward would have meant handing out augmentations bought with a currency that no longer exists. Adramelech surrendered the largest balance by far. Quests now pay into it directly, and high-tier quests pay a great deal.

**Every untrain and reset lockout has been cleared.** Temple of Forgetfulness timers, exhausted gem counts, free skill resets, and the melee/ranged/summoning mastery resets — all wiped for every character. If the recalculation left you wanting to rebuild differently, you can, right now, without waiting out a cooldown. Apex in particular was locked out until the 24th; that is gone.

**Two-handed weapons: cleave no longer pays skill experience.** A two-handed weapon really does swing twice, and it still earns full experience for both strikes. What it no longer earns is experience for every extra creature the cleave clips — that was scaling with how many things happened to be standing near you, which meant two-handers gained several times faster than everyone else for reasons nobody designed. Damage is completely unchanged; you still cleave, it still hurts, it just no longer pays experience per bystander.

**Experience rates are slower overall, deliberately.** The old pace put a character at the level cap in a couple of hundred hours, which was never the intent. If you use a two-handed weapon you will feel this more than most — you are taking both the rate change and the cleave change together.

**All of this is tunable and I expect to tune it.** The rates, the two-handed factors and the pace are all live settings I can change without a restart, and I would rather correct them from real data than guess twice. **Tell me how it feels** — especially if you use a two-handed weapon, because your experience of this update is genuinely different from everyone else's and I want to hear both.

As always — #bugs for anything broken, or just tell me.
