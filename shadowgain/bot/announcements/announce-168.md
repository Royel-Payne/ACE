# Update — Magic and Missile Defence now train when you lose the roll

**Resisting a spell trained your Magic Defence. Failing to resist trained nothing.** That sounds
reasonable until you look at what it means: the chance of a successful resist *is* your Magic
Defence, so the skill only grew once it already worked. The lower yours was, the less often you were
paid to raise it.

The numbers were worse than they look. Against a typical caster, someone with a low Magic Defence
resists roughly **one spell in two hundred** — and only that one paid anything. Someone who had
already built it up resists most spells, and gets paid for most of them.

**From now on, failing the roll pays too — a smaller share, but not nothing.** You rolled your
defence and lost; the skill was still used. The same applies to **Missile Defence** when an arrow
lands on you.

**This is a floor, not a ceiling.** If you have already built these skills up, you will barely
notice — you were being paid for most of your rolls already. If you have been trying to raise one
and getting nowhere, that is the case this changes.

**Melee Defence is deliberately untouched.** It was never the one struggling: things swing at you
constantly, so it already trains at many times the rate of the other two. Nothing about it changes.

**Nothing was taken away, and nothing was nerfed.** Successful resists and evades pay exactly what
they always did. This only adds a payment where there used to be none — and we have started it low
on purpose, so it can be raised once we see how it lands.
