# Update — the two paths stop feeding each other

**Fellowship and allegiance experience now stays on the path it was earned on.** Sharing between a hard-lane character and a fast-lane one was passing large amounts across the line in both directions — which quietly undid the thing the two paths exist to keep separate. From now on, a share counts for the path it came from: hard-lane experience builds hard-lane characters, fast-lane experience builds fast-lane ones.

**Nothing changes when everyone is on the same path.** Two hard-lane characters grouped together share exactly as they always have, and so do two fast-lane characters. Allegiances work the same way. If you have never fellowshipped across the line, you will not notice this at all.

**Why it mattered more than it looks.** An even-share fellowship does not split experience up — it scales it and gives every member the whole amount, so three people in a fellowship put well over double the experience into the group. Above level 50 the usual level-range limits stop applying, which is exactly the range where the Honour Roll and the level-275 role live. A fellowship could carry someone to the top of that list without them having walked the road it is meant to mark.

**Allegiance pass-up costs nobody anything.** The experience your patron receives from you is *generated on top* of yours — it was never taken out of your pocket, and it still is not. If you and your patron are on different paths, that share is simply no longer created.

**Nobody lost a rank, an attribute point, or any experience.** This changes what future sharing is worth, not anything you already have.
