# Update — your attributes now count toward your level, and Loyalty stops running away

**Attributes have not been counting toward your character level, and that was a bug.** Since the last update your level was supposed to come from everything you do — every skill you use *and* every attribute you raise. The skill half worked. The attribute half never did: the experience went into your attributes, your ranks went up, and your level quietly ignored it. Measured across everyone playing, **about a fifth of all the experience being earned was earning no level at all**, and for some of you it was more than half.

**That is fixed, so levels will advance noticeably faster from now on — roughly 20%.** This is not a boost and nothing was made more generous. It is the pace the update was designed to have, finally being delivered. If it feels quicker than yesterday, that is the bug being gone.

**Nobody loses a level.** Levels were recalculated against the corrected totals and the result is three characters up and nobody down. The gap only started when the last update shipped, so there was very little to make up — which is exactly why it was worth fixing immediately rather than letting it pile up.

**Your unassigned experience pool was NOT reset this time.** Last update it was, for good reasons that applied once. Quest and pass-up experience you have earned since then is untouched.

**Loyalty and Leadership have been slowed down hard, and this one costs two people something real.** These are passive skills — you earn Loyalty by being sworn and passing experience up, not by doing anything — and now that level is built from skill experience, they were on course to become a fast route to levels for characters who were barely playing. Two things had already been fixed quietly: monarchs used to be paid Loyalty on their own earnings despite passing nothing up, and kill experience used to flow up the allegiance tree. What was left still had to go.

**So Loyalty and Leadership now grow very slowly at high rank — and normally at low rank.** This matters and it is deliberate: if you are new, or your Loyalty is low, you will still move it at a reasonable pace, because early ranks are cheap. If your Loyalty is already enormous, it has effectively stopped. These skills should never have been anyone's fastest progression, and now they cannot be.

**Adra and Black Breath: your Loyalty skill has gone down.** Adra by 29 points, Black Breath by 11. Those points came from experience banked back when the monarch bug was paying you double. Your level was never calculated from them — the recalculation always used the lower figure — so trimming the skill just brings it into line with the number your level has been using all along. **No level is lost over it.** It is the only number going backwards in this update and I would rather name it than have you find it.

**Everything else is untouched.** Every skill, rank, attribute and item is where you left it. Experience rates for normal play are unchanged from yesterday — the speed-up is the attribute fix, nothing else.

As always — #bugs for anything broken, or just tell me. Especially tell me how the pace feels now: it is the first time it has actually been running as designed, and I would rather correct it from what you report than from what I assume.
