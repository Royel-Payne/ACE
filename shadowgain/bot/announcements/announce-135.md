# Something new — your character, on the web

**https://my.shadowgain.com** — log in with your game account.

It shows your character the way the server actually sees it: **true ranks** for attributes and skills, carrying on past the point the client stops counting, with what you have spent and what the next point costs. Titles, quests, and your whole inventory with the game's own icons.

**And your character in 3D**, wearing exactly what you are wearing right now. Drag to turn it.

**It is read-only.** Nothing on that site can change your character — it only ever reads, and it cannot write to the game at all.

A weekend side project. Found something off? `/bug` here, or `@bug` in game.
